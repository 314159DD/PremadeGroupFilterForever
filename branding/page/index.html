What the listing says

Short and plain: what the addon is and what you get.

  • Solo players: tanks and healers only: hides every solo player who is not marked as tank or healer.
  • Has a tank: shows only groups that already have a tank.
  • Has a healer: shows only groups that already have a healer.
  • Free DPS slots: shows only groups with at least 1, 2 or 3 open DPS slots.
  • Hidden counter: the button shows how many entries are filtered out.
